Help Ziad and His Four Children Rebuild Their Lives

My name is Ziad Hassan Radwan, an Arabic language teacher from Gaza.

Today, I am not writing as a teacher. I am writing as a father struggling to protect my children and give them a dignified life.

The war destroyed our home (approximately $150,000) and our farm (approximately $120,000). I lost my savings while trying to survive.

For months, my family and I have been living in a rented home, paying $700 every month just to have a roof over our heads. I continued working as a teacher, but 20% of my salary was cut. I am now struggling with debt and the high cost of food, water, electricity, transportation, rent and education.

My four children

  • My daughter is studying Dentistry, but her education is at risk.
  • My son is studying Multimedia & Web Development at university.
  • My third son is in 11th grade.
  • My youngest child is in 2nd grade.
